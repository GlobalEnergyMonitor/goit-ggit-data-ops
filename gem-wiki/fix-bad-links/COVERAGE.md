**Scope rule (2026-07-28).** Scope is every country in the assignments sheet.

data update, not whether the wiki's Background citations still resolve. Batches

Germany unswept — corrected in the 2026-07-28 batch. Page lists come from the
**union of the wiki category and the GEM export's `Wiki` column**; the category
alone under-counts (see SCOPE.md).
