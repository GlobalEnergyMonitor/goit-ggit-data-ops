## P0448: IGAT 9 Gas Pipeline (Asaluyeh to Bazargan)

Tracker says: construction since 2022, NIGC 100%, 1,863 km, 56 in, 3,885 MMcf/d (39.7 bcm/y), start year blank, Delayed yes. Researcher note: "still under construction as of December 2025".

What I found:
- The line is being built and commissioned in sections. A Shana report of 19 February 2026 says 310 km of the Ahvaz-Kouhdasht pipeline went into operation during the Ten-Day Dawn celebrations (February 2026). https://en.shana.ir/news/1707000/Shana (Shana, 19 Feb 2026, opened).
- Another Shana item, 11 February 2026, lists commissioned sections of the Ninth National Gas Pipeline: Ahvaz-Shoushtar 114 km, Shoushtar-Dezful 96 km, Pol-e Dokhtar-Kouhdasht 103 km. It also says Ajabshir-Marand, about 260 km in 48 and 50 in, was approved to continue. No completion date. https://en.shana.ir/news/1605363/Shana (Shana, 11 Feb 2026, opened; read through a fetch tool summary).
- The Dezful-Pol-e Dokhtar section is well behind schedule, blamed on the contractor. Construction began in late 1401 (2022-23). The company says the other three sections of Ahvaz-Kouhdasht are not delayed. https://www.mehrnews.com/news/6689516/ (Mehr News, 15 Dec 2025, opened). This is the row's own Status and Construction reference. It supports "construction" and the 2022 start. It gives no lengths.
- Miandoab-Ajabshir, 56 in, nearly 99 km, 98 million cubic meters per day design, was 96% complete in mid-June 2026 with commissioning scheduled by late November 2026. https://en.shana.ir/news/2178894/Shana (Shana, 14 Jun 2026, opened).
- A Mehr/Tejarat item reported that the 95 km Miandoab-Ajabshir part reached 80% in 11 months (2025). Snippet only, via search result, Mehr News 13 Khordad 1404 (June 2025), not opened.
- Iranian reports disagree on total length: 1,750 km Asaluyeh to Bazargan, about 1,200 km Bidboland to Bazargan, 1,900 km in other coverage. Snippet only (search summaries, Persian and English). The row's 1,863 km cites a JICA PDF that I did not open.

What I could not find: any report of IGAT 9 damage from the March 2026 strikes. No 2026 report with a full completion date for the whole line. The Capacity source (BNC Network) was not opened.

Verdict: needs researcher check.

Suggested change: keep the whole-line status as construction, but split by section if the tracker allows it. Mark Ahvaz-Shoushtar, Shoushtar-Dezful, Pol-e Dokhtar-Kouhdasht (about 313 km) and the 310 km Ahvaz-Kouhdasht piece as operating. Note the overlap between these two figures: the 310 km operating claim and the three sections (about 313 km) may be the same pipe, so do not add them. Miandoab-Ajabshir (about 99 km) is a likely operating row by December 2026. Dezful-Pol-e Dokhtar stays construction, delayed. Ajabshir-Marand (about 260 km) is approved and not shown to be started, so it is probably proposed. Recheck total length against NIGC data before keeping 1,863 km. Add a start year for the sections that have dates.

## P0766: Turkmenistan-Afghanistan-Pakistan-India (TAPI)

Tracker says: construction (Afghan work from 2024), Turkmengaz 85%, Afghan Gas 5%, Interstate Gas Systems 5%, GAIL 5%, 33 bcm/y, 1,814 km, 1,420 mm, Galkynysh to Fazilka. Start year blank, Delayed yes.

Physically built, with dates:
- Pipe laid in Herat Province by date: 25 km by 31 March 2026 (Afghanistan International, opened). 64 km by 20 May 2026 (Pajhwok, snippet only). 80 km by 9 June 2026 (Pajhwok, page opened but only navigation text came back, so snippet only). 116 km by early August 2026, 38 km underground, another 135 km prepared (Interfax, 11 Aug 2026, opened). 122.3 km welded and laid, 71.5 km buried, 33 km hydro-tested, as of 15 September 2026 (Times of Central Asia, 16 Sep 2026, opened). Turkmen officials in late October: 150.7 km right-of-way prepared, 124 km welded and assembled, about 80%, hydro-testing under way (Eurasianet, 13 Nov 2026, opened via Wayback). Satellite images on 7 November show pipe reaching a point about 22 km west of Herat's industrial district (RFE/RL, 10 Nov 2026, opened).
- The built section is 153 km from Serhetabat on the Turkmen border to Herat. Turkmengaz says the Herat stage will be finished by end of 2026. A Shamshad News headline of 16 June 2026 says gas reaches Herat in early 2027 (snippet only).
- Nothing built beyond Herat. Turkmengaz's deputy chair said in early October that Kandahar is a priority for 2027 (Eurasianet, 13 Nov 2026). RFE/RL says India appeared to have turned away from TAPI by 2024 and Pakistan's interest waned. No work found in Pakistan or India.
- Funding: Turkmen government, reportedly about US$600 million for this section (Eurasianet).
- A memorandum on Herat gasification was signed in August 2026 (Interfax, 11 Aug 2026). This is a ceremony, not construction.

Row problems:
- Length 1,814 km is the whole design route. About 124 km of it is welded. The tracker mixes planned and built.
- The Status source jamestown.org and pipeline-journal.net were not opened. The oilgas.gov.tm and worldpipelines URLs were not opened.
- Capacity 33 bcm/y and ADB URL: not opened. 33 bcm/y is the long-standing design figure.

Verdict: needs researcher check. Construction is correct only for the Serhetabat-Herat section.

Suggested change: split TAPI into Serhetabat-Herat (153 km, construction, start year 2026 or early 2027) and the rest (Herat to Fazilka, about 1,660 km, proposed or shelved, since there is no physical work and India and Pakistan have stepped back). Keep the 2024 construction year, since Interfax says work on the Afghan stretch began on 11 September 2024 (from search result, snippet only). If the tracker keeps one row, lower the commissioning claim to the Afghan section only and add a note on km laid. The Turkmen stretch from Galkynysh to the border was built earlier. I did not verify it, so check it as part of the split.

## P7104: Russia-Iran Gas Pipeline (55 bcm/y)

Tracker says: construction, 2025, owners Iran's Ministry of Petroleum and Gazprom, 55 bcm/y, 1,413.38 km merged length, no endpoints. Status sources: intellinews and a TASS item (neither opened).

What I found:
- No construction has started. Supplies would use existing Soviet-era lines through Azerbaijan to Astara. A CACI Analyst piece of 24 July 2026 says imports have not begun, the contract is not final, and no new pipeline is reported built or contracted. It lists Novo-Filya to Baku (200 km, 10 bcm/y) and Hajigabul to Astara to Abadan (1,474.5 km, 10 bcm/y, inactive). https://cacianalyst.org/publications/analytical-articles/item/13981-from-soviet-pipelines-to-eurasian-energy-corridors-russian-gas-exports-to-iran-through-azerbaijan.html (CACI Analyst, 24 Jul 2026, opened).
- On 24 June 2026 NIGC's head said only the gas price and payment mechanism remain open, in a two-stage program. https://interfax.com/newsroom/top-stories/118194/ (Interfax, 24 Jun 2026, opened).
- On 13 February 2026 Iran's ambassador said a contract is being prepared, and that pipeline construction "will probably take two to three years" after signing. First stage supply via existing infrastructure up to 1.8 bcm/y. https://interfax.com/newsroom/top-stories/116131/ (Interfax, 13 Feb 2026, opened).
- On 18 February 2026 Iran's oil minister said Iran is still looking for ways to import Russian gas. https://interfax.com/newsroom/top-stories/116227/ (Interfax, 18 Feb 2026, opened).
- Another snippet: roic.ai, 26 Aug 2026, "Iran and Russia edge closer to landmark gas deal". Fetch returned 403. Snippet only.

The 1,413.38 km length is not backed by any source I found and the row has no length reference. The 55 bcm/y is the potential, not a built or contracted figure. The first phase is about 2 bcm/y over existing pipe.

Verdict: likely wrong.

Suggested change: change status from construction to proposed. Drop the 2025 construction year. Add a note that supply would use existing pipelines through Azerbaijan, with no new build contracted. Re-examine the length and the 55 bcm/y capacity, which are not backed.

## P2425: Pochinki-Anapa Gas Pipeline

Tracker says: construction since 2018, start year 2023, Delayed yes, Gazprom 100%, 1,625 km, 63 bcm/y, 1,420 mm. Researcher note: construction ongoing (8/20/25).

What I found:
- On 25 December 2025 Gazprom's chief Alexei Miller said Gazprom "completed an important stage" and commissioned new sections of Pochinki-Anapa with a total length of 616 km, supplying southern Russia and the North Caucasus. https://www.interfax.ru/russia/1065146 (Interfax, 25 Dec 2025, opened). The same page gives the full route length as 1,625.6 km and says the purpose is domestic supply.
- A Krasnodar planning order of 3 February 2026 approves territory planning for the Pochinki-Anapa section km 1231 to km 1379, so planning for further sections was still open in 2026. https://xn-----6kcbamkjdqxosfkgrcv9ae.xn--p1ai/item/2430851 (Neznaimanovskoe rural settlement administration, 3 Feb 2026, opened, title and order text only).
- Interfax of 19 July 2019 (the row's Construction reference) confirms the 1,625.6 km length and that more than 500 km was built by 2018. It gives no capacity. The 63 bcm/y does not appear there. https://www.interfax.ru/business/669751 (Interfax, 19 Jul 2019, opened).
- Neftegaz item (design documentation approved, km 963.7 to 1168.1, 204.4 km): the page returned no text. Only a search snippet, so it is snippet only. The gem.wiki page and the Gazprom tender spreadsheet were not opened.

What I could not find: which sections make up the 616 km, whether the whole line is now done, and any 2026 completion date for the rest. About 1,000 km of the 1,625.6 km is not shown as commissioned.

The row's start year (2023) has passed.

Verdict: needs researcher check.

Suggested change: split the row. Mark the sections Gazprom said it commissioned in 2025 (616 km, start year 2025) as operating. Keep the remaining sections as construction (or proposed where only planning exists, such as km 1231 to 1379), with no start year until Gazprom gives one. Ask the researcher to find the section list from Gazprom's 2025 results or the investment program. Check the 63 bcm/y capacity. The Southern Corridor was originally sized at 63 bcm/y for the whole system and the 2019 article does not support it for this line alone.

## P5788: Beineu-Bozoy-Shymkent Gas Pipeline, Segment II (second string, stage 1)

Tracker says: construction since 2025, start 2026-12, QazaqGaz and UCC Holding, 1,449 km, 10 bcm/y, 1,067 mm. Related rows: P3273 segment I operating (2013), P6712 segment II capacity expansion proposed (2029, length 0).

What I found:
- Construction start: Kazakh deputy PM-office press conference said the second string began construction in April 2025, with commissioning planned by end of 2026. https://primeminister.kz/ru/news/rost-v-obrabatyvayushchey-promyshlennosti-vvod-novykh-proektov-gazifikatsiya-i-razvitie-transportnoy-infrastruktury-sostoyalas-press-konferentsiya-s-uchastiem-romana-sklyaranbsp-30347 (Prime Minister's office, mid-2025, opened). This is the row's own Status and Start reference.
- Progress as of 7 July 2026: basic design done, surveys 98%, overall design about 36%, equipment ordered, first pipes at the Chinese border, pipe distribution along the route scheduled for early August and welding mid-August, first compressor units leave the factory in September. https://bizmedia.kz/2026-07-07-kazahstan-stroit-tri-gpz-i-nitku-gazoprovoda/ (Bizmedia, 7 Jul 2026, opened) and https://www.trend.az/casia/kazakhstan/4204682.html (Trend, 7 Jul 2026, opened).
- Energy minister, at a July 2026 government meeting: the linear part of the first phase will be finished by end of 2026, and the second phase in 2029 raises annual transmission to 15 bcm. https://qazinform.com/news/kazakhstan-unveils-timeline-for-new-gas-processing-plants-aadac1 (Kazinform, July 2026, opened, date not shown on the page; matches bes.media of 7 Jul 2026, https://bes.media/news/gazifikatsiyu-kazahstana-hotyat-dovesti-do-80-k-2035-god/, opened).
- Later: on 16 October 2026 the prime minister's office said design and delivery of equipment and materials are ongoing, with stage 1 scheduled for 2027 and stage 2 for 2029. https://daryo.uz/2026/10/16/kazakhstan-qatars-pih-advance-gas-processing-projects-at-kashagan/ (Daryo, 16 Oct 2026, from the official PM source, opened).
- A Prime article of 3 November 2026 says construction and installation work has started and gives the length as about 1,450 km. No commissioning date. https://1prime.ru/20261103/zavod-874892513.html (Prime, 3 Nov 2026, opened). It states about 15 bcm/y for the existing first string, which does not match the tracker's 10 bcm/y for segment II.
- No report I found confirms that pipe was actually welded or laid in the ground. The 2026 evidence is plans, deliveries and a general "installation has started".
- The inbusiness.kz item about China not approving the design is from 13 July 2024. It is old, so it is not a current risk signal.

Mismatches:
- Start 2026-12 matches the end-2026 target for the linear part of stage 1, but the October 2026 statement says stage 1 in 2027. Design was only 36% done in July 2026, so December 2026 looks unlikely for commissioning.
- Capacity: 10 bcm/y for stage 1. Official reports say 15 bcm/y after stage 2. The adilet decree in the row was not opened. Check what stage 1 adds. The Prime article and the minister's comments do not give a stage 1 figure.
- Length 1,449 km is the same as the first string, which is normal for a parallel line.
- The kase.kz KazTransGas PDF and the ortcom.kz page (503) were not opened.

Verdict: needs researcher check. Construction is defensible, because pipe, compressors and contracts are in progress. Evidence of laid pipe is missing.

Suggested change: change StartYear1 from 2026-12 to 2027, per the 16 October 2026 statement, and mark Delayed. Check capacity for stage 1. Look for QazaqGaz or contractor (Kazakhstan contract lots 370-730 km and 730-1,090 km are listed on mcc.kz, snippet only) news that shows km welded before the next release.
