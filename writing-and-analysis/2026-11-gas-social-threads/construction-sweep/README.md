# Status sweep of the longest gas pipelines in construction

Checked 2026-11-19. Scope: every row with status construction for the 15 pipeline names in the three top 10 tables of the "Longest gas pipelines under construction, Nov 2026" Sheet. That is 73 rows. Values were read from the live backend Sheet on 2026-11-19, not the Nov 5 release file. Nothing in the backend was changed.

The detail for each row, with sources, is in the five files in this folder. Each file is split by region. The key claims for the Xinjiang coal-to-gas line, the four India lines, Pochinki-Anapa and Russia-Iran were checked a second time against the source itself.

## Main finding

The top 10 lists overstate what is being built. Most of the longest "construction" rows put a whole route length under construction when only part is being built, or when most of it already operates. Fixing the rows below would take well over 10,000 km out of the 69,597 km world construction total, and it would change most of both top 10 lists.

## Rows that need a researcher check

- MGS III overall is right as construction, with a target of the first half of 2028. Jeddah (P6736) may not be awarded yet. Sinopec's packages (P6723 to P6726) have a contract date of May 2027, against 2028 in the tracker. Aramco gives an added capacity of 3.15 billion cubic feet a day for the whole program, which could go on the system row.
- Anhui and Guizhou networks: weak evidence. Chinese search was mostly blocked, and almost every finding rests on search snippets. Probable problems: Hefei-Chaohu (P5636) has operated since March 2026. Huanchaohu (P7526) double counts P5636 and P7527. Eastern North-South Backbone (P4583) looks like a copy of the whole Northeast Anhui line. Part of Jinsha-Qianxi-Nayong (P4956) will not be built. Five Qiandongnan lines were planned to operate in 2024 or 2025. P7492 lists Sonatrach as owner. P4992, P7524 and P4980 have capacities that do not fit. 19 of the 31 rows were not checked at all.
- Lunnan-Shangku (P7116) was due to be linked up by November 2025. No commissioning report was found.

## Rows that look right

Xinjiang coal-to-gas Zhundong branch (P4749, due late 2026, so check again in early 2027). Sichuan-Shanghai second line: Wuhu branch (P4659), Wenzhou-Fuding (P4665), Zaoyang-Xuancheng (P4666) and Tongliang-Qianjiang (P7568), with a target of 2027. All other MGS III segments.
