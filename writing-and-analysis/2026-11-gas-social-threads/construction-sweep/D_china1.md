## Xinjiang Coal-to-Gas Pipeline (Zhundong coal-to-gas export line)

Context: PipeChina's West Pipeline Company is building the "Zhundong coal-to-gas pipeline" (准东煤制天然气管道). Planned total length 780 km in three phases. Phase 1 is one trunk and two branches, 6 bcm/y (60亿立方米/年), pressure 12 MPa.

### P4749 Zhundong Branch (114 km, 6 bcm/y)

- Tracker: construction, ConstructionYear 2025, StartYear blank, 114.00 km, 6.00 bcm/y, DN610, Xiheishan/Jiangjunmiao to Jijihu, owner a PipeChina Xinjiang coal-to-gas export company.
- Found: The two branches are Jiangjunmiao-Jijihu and Xiheishan-Jijihu. Total 114.118 km, 6 bcm/y system capacity, DN610 inner automatic welding. Welding began 2025-03-19. Plan was "built and ready for commissioning by 2026-10-30". The Xinhua roundup of 2026-11-10 covers the trunk (welding over 94%) and does not mention the branches. I found no commissioning report for the branches.
- Verdict: correct (construction).
- Suggested change: set StartYear1 to 2026 (planned, not yet confirmed). Recheck in Dec 2026 to Jan 2027 because the branch target date has just passed. Owner: the source names only PipeChina West Pipeline Company as builder. The project-company owner in the tracker could not be checked here. Researcher may confirm.
- Sources (all opened):
  - Science and Technology Daily (科技日报), 2025-03-22, "新疆准东煤制天然气管道支线工程开工" https://www.stdaily.com/web/gdxw/2025-03/22/content_313291.html. Gloss: branches Jiangjunmiao-Jijihu and Xiheishan-Jijihu, 114.118 km, 6 bcm/y, to be built by 2026-10-30.
  - Xinhua, 2026-11-10, "国家管网集团油气基础设施建设提速" https://www.news.cn/energy/20261110/c74c9f20138e4c1db6df7fa622d2f518/c.html. Gloss: Zhundong trunk (Jijihu-Liaodun) welding above 94%, Tianshan tunnel No. 2 broke through 6 Oct. Branches not mentioned.

### P4747 Main Line (4,159 km, 30 bcm/y, DN1219, start Mulei, StartYear 2022, Delayed yes)

- Tracker: construction, 4,159 km, 30 bcm/y, 1,219 mm, start location "Mulei" (木垒), StartYear1 2022, delayed. Status ref is a Toutiao article (Wayback copy not archived; I could not open it).
- Found: The line actually under construction is the Phase 1 trunk, Jijihu first station (Mulei County) to Liaodun compressor station (Yizhou District, Hami), about 254 km, DN914, 12 MPa, 3 stations and 8 valve chambers. It joins West-East Line 4 at Liaodun. Construction began 2025-09-09. Target completion end of 2026. Welding over 94% by 2026-11-10. Phase 1 capacity is 6 bcm/y, shared with the two branches. Nothing I opened mentions a 4,159 km, 30 bcm/y, DN1219 main line under construction. That looks like the older long-distance plan (the branches listed in the sibling rows are mostly cancelled).
- Verdict: likely wrong. The status "construction" is right for the Phase 1 trunk, but length, capacity, diameter and start year describe something else.
- Suggested change: researcher should decide whether this row is the Phase 1 trunk (254 km, 914 mm, 6 bcm/y, Jijihu to Liaodun, StartYear 2026 or 2027, no longer "delayed since 2022") or the old 4,159 km scheme. If it is the old scheme it is not under construction and should be proposed or shelved, with the 254 km trunk added or relabeled as the construction row. Also note the 254 km trunk has no row of its own that I could see in the input.
- Sources:
  - Science and Technology Daily, 2025-09-09, "我国最大煤制天然气基地外输管道开工" https://www.stdaily.com/web/gdxw/2025-09/09/content_398057.html (opened). Gloss: 780 km planned in three phases; Phase 1 trunk Jijihu to Liaodun about 254 km, DN914; completion expected end 2026.
  - People's Daily Energy supplement, 2025-09-15 https://paper.people.com.cn/zgnyb/pc/content/202509/15/content_30105386.html (opened, same text).
  - Xinhua 2026-11-10 (above).
  - Tracker status ref (Toutiao Wayback copy): not opened. Wayback returned "has not archived that URL".

## Sichuan-Shanghai Parallel Gas Pipeline (川气东送二线)

Project facts (opened sources): 4,269 km, 8 provinces, two parts. West section (川渝鄂段, Sichuan-Chongqing-Hubei) started construction in 2023. East section (鄂豫赣皖浙闽段) is 2,698 km with one trunk, 2 connector lines and 3 branch lines. Whole line expected to be complete and operating in 2027.

Commissioned so far (opened sources):
- 2025-08-25: Anyue gas field upload point to Tongliang compressor station, 56.15 km, about 3 bcm/y added (China Daily 2025-08-25, Xinhua/CCTV via Huanqiu).
- 2025-12-29: Sichuan section, about 190 km, designed 20 bcm/y, gas from Weiyuan and Luxian fields (Xinhua via People's Political Consultative Conference site, 2026-01-04).
- 2026-11-10: year-to-date welding nearly 800 km, about 80% of 2026 target of 1,000 km, and the Qingshanhe directional drill is done for the Hechuan supply branch (Xinhua).
- East section: tunnel works still going in Zhejiang (Oct 2026). I found no commissioning of any east-section segment.

### P5678 Weiyuan/Lu County-Tongliang Trunk Line (282.77 km, 20 bcm/y)

- Tracker: construction, ConstructionYear 2023, StartYear blank, 282.77 km, 20 bcm/y, Weiyuan County to Tongliang District.
- Found: This row is stage 1 of the west section in the Ministry of Ecology and Environment (MEE) review notice: Weiyuan/Luxian to the Tongliang-Hechuan boundary, 282.77 km (81.52 km DN1016 plus 201.25 km DN1219), 20 bcm/y, shale gas from Weiyuan and Luxian. Length and capacity match. The Sichuan section (about 190 km, 20 bcm/y) went into operation on 2025-12-29. The remaining roughly 93 km is in Chongqing (Dazu, Tongnan, Tongliang). Also the 56.15 km Anyue-Tongliang compressor station section went live 2025-08-25, which probably sits on or next to this route. I could not find a report saying the Chongqing part is finished.
- Verdict: likely wrong (at least partly operating).
- Suggested change: researcher should split the row or move it to operating with StartYear 2025 (first gas 2025-08 and 2025-12), after confirming which km the 190 km and the 56.15 km cover. If the Chongqing part is not confirmed, keep a construction row only for the unfinished part. Note the 190 km figure is from Xinhua, not from a PipeChina notice I opened.
- Sources (opened):
  - Xinhua via People's Political Consultative Conference site (人民政协网), 2026-01-04, "川气东送二线四川段进气投产" https://www.rmzxw.com.cn/c/2026-01-04/3843954.shtml. Gloss: "On 29 Dec the Sichuan section went into operation, about 190 km, designed 20 bcm/y... gas from Weiyuan, Luxian fields."
  - People's Daily (张伟, PipeChina chairman), 2026-01-16, via rmlt.com.cn https://www.rmlt.com.cn/2026/0122/746004.shtml. Gloss: Sichuan section built and operating; 2026 welding target over 1,000 km.
  - China Daily, 2025-08-25, "首段投产！川气东送二线建设全面提速" https://cn.chinadaily.com.cn/a/202508/25/WS68ac0be0a3104ba1353fe99f.html. Gloss: first section Anyue upload point to Tongliang compressor station, 56.15 km, about 3 bcm/y.
  - MEE review notice, 2023-07-26, stage 1 environmental assessment https://www.mee.gov.cn/ywgz/hjyxpj/jsxmhjyxpj/nscxmgs/202307/t20230726_1037259.shtml. Gloss: 282.77 km, 20 bcm/y, investment 6.123 billion yuan, builder PipeChina Southwest Pipeline Company.

### P4663 Xiantao-Wenzhou Trunk Line (1,260 km, 27 bcm/y)

- Tracker: construction, 1,260 km, capacity 27.00 bcm/y, DN1016/1219, Xiantao to Wenzhou.
- Found: Length 1,260 km and diameters are right. Capacity is wrong. The MEE approval (2025-01-06) gives Xiantao-Xuancheng 691.55 km, DN1016, 130亿 m3/y (13 bcm/y) and Xuancheng-Wenzhou 568.45 km, DN1219, 140亿 m3/y (14 bcm/y). The tracker's 27 looks like 13 plus 14 added together. A 2025-02 CCTV report says the whole east section is designed at 140亿 m3/y.
- Verdict: status correct (construction, target 2027). Capacity likely wrong.
- Suggested change: change capacity to 14 bcm/y (or 13 for the Xiantao-Xuancheng part if the row is split). Set StartYear1 2027 (planned).
- Sources (opened): MEE approval of the east-section environmental report, 2025-01-06 https://www.mee.gov.cn/xxgk2018/xxgk/xxgk11/202501/t20250106_1100014.html. ## Xinjiang Gas Pipeline Network

### P5719 Tacheng Area Gas Pipeline (264 km, 0.5 bcm/y)

- Tracker: construction, ProposalYear 2022, StartYear blank, 264 km, 0.5 bcm/y, Baijiantan to Tacheng.
- Found: Tacheng prefecture "natural gas benefit-the-people project, phase 1" was finished and put into operation on 2025-12-27 (Tacheng prefecture government site, 2025-12-29). Pipeline 398 km, one trunk and two branches, from Karamay first station through Toli County Tiechanggou, Emin County, Tacheng city to Baketu terminal, 7 stations and 10 valve chambers, 5亿 m3/y (0.5 bcm/y). Earlier local news gave about 407 km total (279 km trunk plus 128 km branches), project started Oct 2023 per one page and Oct 2022 per another, 5亿 m3/y, and the tracker's own archived source (April 2025 copy) says 407 km. None of them says 264 km. Capacity 0.5 matches.
- Verdict: likely wrong. Status should be operating, and length should be about 398 km (or 407 km, official figures differ).
- Suggested change: status to operating, StartYear1 2025 (month 12), length 398 km (note the sources differ between 397 and 407 km). Owner: sources name the Tacheng prefecture state-owned assets investment and operating group, which is not the same name as the tracker's "Tacheng Area Natural Gas Investment and Development Co Ltd". Researcher should check the company name.
- Sources (opened):
  - Tacheng prefecture government, 2025-12-29, "美丽塔城2025｜晒晒幸福事儿 塔城通管道天然气啦" https://www.xjtc.gov.cn/ywdt/jrtc/jjdt/content_58764. Gloss: "On 27 Dec the Tacheng natural gas project (phase 1) was completed and gas supplied... pipeline 398 km... 5亿 m3 a year."
  - Tacheng prefecture government, 2025-05-16, project in final stage, about 407 km https://www.xjtc.gov.cn/ywdt/jrtc/jjdt/content_55194.
  - Tracker's own source, Wayback copy of 2025-02-24 article https://web.archive.org/web/20250421013424/https://www.xjtc.gov.cn/ywdt/jrtc/jjdt/content_53425. Gloss: 407 km, trunk 279 km, branches 128 km, 5亿 m3/y, construction began Oct 2022 (the May 2025 article says Oct 2023).

### P7115 Yingmaili-Sancha Gas Pipeline (382.5 km, 7.2 million m3/day)

- Tracker: construction, 2025, StartYear blank, 382.5 km, 7.2 million Sm3/d (2.63 bcm/y), owner "--".

- Verdict: likely wrong. Status should be operating.

- Sources (opened): Xinhua Xinjiang channel, 2025-12-02, "塔里木油田气化南疆天然气管道工程英三线投产成功" http://xj.news.cn/20251202/82769c42ea694d93b9fd3cd24f4af0e5/c.html. ### P7116 Lunnan-Shangku Gas Pipeline (128 km, 20.4 million m3/day)

- Tracker: construction, 2025, StartYear blank, 128 km, 20.4 million Sm3/d (7.45 bcm/y).
- Found: Started with Yingmaili-Sancha on 2025-03-10. Original plan was to link up by 2025-11-30. As of 2025-12-02, Xinhua says first-batch full linkup waits for Lunnan-Shangku to be built and put into operation. I found no report of its commissioning in 2026 (searches in Chinese found only the construction start and plan). 128 km matches the environmental report (93.9 plus 34.1 km). 2040万 m3/day matches the construction-start reports. This is a gap in what I could find, not proof it is still unfinished.
- Verdict: needs researcher check. It is overdue against its own plan, so it may already be operating.
- Suggested change: check Tarim Oilfield or Bayingolin (Korla) news for the Shangku/Lunnan commissioning. If none, keep construction and set StartYear 2026. Owner "--": likely PetroChina Tarim Oilfield Company (not verified).
- Sources (opened): Xinhua Xinjiang 2025-12-02 (above). Xinjiang government, 2024-03-30, project launch https://www.xinjiang.gov.cn/xinjiang/dzdt/202404/91c67e6c14c0453c9e823862d8996e16.shtml.

### P7448 Gasification of Southern Xinjiang gas pipeline (2,108 km, 11.3 bcm/y)

- Tracker: construction, ConstructionYear 2024, 2,108 km, 11.3 bcm/y, no endpoints, owner "--".
- Found: 2,108 km is the planned length of the whole three-batch project. Batch 1 is 616 km, 31 million m3/day (31 million m3/d = 11.3 bcm/y). So the capacity belongs to batch 1 only, while the length is for all three batches. Batch 1 is made of the Kela-Yingmaili pipe (P7114, operating Oct 2024), Yingmaili-Sancha (P7115) and Lunnan-Shangku (P7116). So this umbrella row double counts those three. Batches 2 and 3 (Sancha to Zepu, Hotan, Qiemo, Ruoqiang etc.) are "to be launched at a suitable time"; I found no start for them.
- Verdict: needs researcher check.
- Suggested change: remove the double counting. Either make P7448 cover only batches 2 and 3 as proposed (length about 2,108 minus 616 km, capacity unknown), or retire it. The 11.3 bcm/y should not sit beside 2,108 km.
- Sources (opened): Xinjiang government (Xinjiang Daily), 2024-03-30, "气化南疆天然气管道工程全面开建" https://www.xinjiang.gov.cn/xinjiang/dzdt/202404/91c67e6c14c0453c9e823862d8996e16.shtml. Gloss: whole project 2,108 km, three batches; batch 1 616 km, 31 million m3/day, plan to finish 2025. Xinhua Xinjiang 2025-12-02 (above): batch 1 Kela line began operating Oct 2024.
