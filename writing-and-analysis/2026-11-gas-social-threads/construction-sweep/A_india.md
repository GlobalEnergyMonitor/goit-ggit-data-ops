# India gas pipelines with status "construction": fact-check (2026-11-19)

Read-only research. Nothing was edited. Every URL below was opened (by WebFetch, curl, or a downloaded PDF) unless marked "snippet only".

Key regulator source used throughout: the PNGRB (Petroleum and Natural Gas Regulatory Board) monthly pipeline report for August 2026, with data as on June 2026. File: https://pngrb.gov.in/data-bank/20260831-NGPL-MIS-Report.pdf (PNGRB, PDF created 2026-10-16, downloaded and read). The tracker's own "Status [ref]" points to the PNGRB data bank page, which is where this PDF is linked. I also read the June 2025 report (https://pngrb.gov.in/data-bank/20250630-NGPL-MIS-Report.pdf), the August 2025 report (https://pngrb.gov.in/data-bank/20250831-NGPL-MIS-Report.pdf) and the January 2026 tariff table (https://pngrb.gov.in/data-bank/20260113-NGPL-Tariff-MIS.pdf, data as on 31 March 2025).

## 1. P0929 Jagdishpur-Haldia-Bokaro Dhamra-Paradip-Barauni-Guwahati (whole line, no segment)

Tracker says: construction, StartYear1 2025, Delayed yes, 3,546 km, 23 MMSCMD (8.40 bcm/y), owner GAIL Ltd 100%, Jagdishpur to Haldia. LastUpdated 2026-08-28. No refs on length, capacity or start.

What I found:
- **Most of the line already operates.** PNGRB (August 2026 report, data as on June 2026) shows 3,315 km operating out of 3,546 km authorized, with 231 km still under construction. That is about 93 percent operating.
- GAIL's own figure (press release 9 April 2025, https://gailonline.com/PressRelease_09042025.html, opened): the integrated line including Barauni-Guwahati is 3,306 km authorized. 3,227 km laid and 3,119 km in commercial operation at that date. It was carrying 12.26 MMSCMD.
- **Operating sections:** Phulpur-Dobhi-Bokaro-Durgapur, Bokaro-Angul-Dhamra, Dobhi-Barauni-Guwahati. Durgapur to Kolkata (132 km of the 294 km Durgapur-Haldia section) was in commercial operation in April 2025 (same GAIL release).
- **Barauni-Guwahati (about 727 km route):** inaugurated by the Prime Minister in March 2024. As of the Assam Tribune report of 20 May 2025 (https://assamtribune.com/guwahati/one-year-after-pm-inaugurates-it-guwahati-barauni-gas-pipeline-stuck-at-panikhaiti-1578060, opened), 717 km of 727 km was commissioned, 2.5 km from Panikhaiti to Noonmati was not, and 9 km more was under construction. The paper quotes contractor problems and gives no target date. I found no 2026 update on this stub.
- **Parts still open:** Kolkata-Haldia (162 km, of which 103 km was laid in April 2025) and Dhamra-Haldia (240 km authorized, 198 km laid in April 2025). In GAIL's March 2026 filing, as reported by PSU Connect (https://www.psuconnect.in/psu-news/gail-india-limited-revises-completion-schedule-for-key-jhbdpl-pipeline-sections, 25 March 2026, opened), Dhamra-Haldia is listed with 150 km laid or commissioned of 240 km authorized. The Durgapur-Haldia line in the same table gives 3,119 km / 3,266 km, which look like whole-network figures put in the wrong row, so do not use that line.
- **Latest target:** GAIL's board moved the Jagdishpur-Haldia-Bokaro-Dhamra and Dhamra-Haldia completion to March 2027 on 30 October 2026, citing no "hindrance-free construction sites". It said no extra capex is needed. Source: Whalesbook, 30 October 2026, https://www.whalesbook.com/corporate-news/English/energy/GAIL-Extends-Pipeline-Project-Completion-Deadlines-to-March-2027/6ae46088c6c6dccbb90c6444 (opened). Earlier the target had been March 2025, then December 2025, then March 2026, then September 2026 (Whalesbook 25 March 2026, https://www.whalesbook.com/corporate-news/English/industrial-goodsservices/GAIL-India-Delays-Pipeline-to-Sept-2026-Over-Land-Fishery-Snags/69c4193d22ed690e87ac8f24, opened; that page warns it carries AI-generated content). On the Q2 FY27 earnings call (reported by Investing.com, 1 November 2026, https://www.investing.com/news/transcripts/earnings-call-transcript-gail-posts-slight-q2-miss-as-volumes-hit-record-93CH-4989513, opened), management said the "remaining section" of JHBDPL would be done in FY 2026-27, so by March 2027.
- Capacity: PNGRB 23.0 MMSCMD in both 2025 and 2026 reports. Matches the tracker.
- Owner: GAIL, matches.
- Length: 3,546 km matches the PNGRB authorized length. GAIL says 3,306 km. The 240 km difference is not explained in any source I opened.

Verdict: **likely wrong** as a single construction row. About 3,315 km (93 percent) is operating, so "construction" for the whole 3,546 km overstates what is left.

Suggested change in plain words:
- Split the row. Put the operating sections in as operating, with their km and real start years. Keep only the unfinished parts as construction: Kolkata-Haldia (about 162 km), Dhamra-Haldia (about 90 km still to finish), and the last 2.5 km of Barauni-Guwahati at Guwahati. The researcher must decide how to split, because I could not find a section-by-section km table.
- If the researcher keeps one row, change StartYear1 from 2025 (already past) to 2027, and add a note that 3,315 km operates.
- Add the missing Capacity, Length and Start refs. The PNGRB August 2026 report and the GAIL April 2025 release back them up.
- Not verified: whether the Dhamra-Haldia and Kolkata-Haldia sections moved from the Sept 2026 to Mar 2027 target in actual commissioning. I found no commissioning news up to 19 November 2026.

Source URL check: the row's only ref (https://pngrb.gov.in/eng-web/data-bank.html) loads (HTTP 200). It is a listing page that links the monthly PDFs, and the August 2026 PDF supports the 3,546 km, 23 MMSCMD and the partial status. The Mar-26 target date in that PDF is stale.

Search-snippet-only claims (not opened): GEM tracker "315 km operational" mentioned in a search summary. That figure disagrees with PNGRB and I could not confirm it. gem.wiki returned 403 to WebFetch, so I did not read the wiki page.

## 2. P1309 North East Natural Gas Pipeline Grid Project (Indradhanush Gas Grid Ltd)

Tracker says: construction, StartYear1 2026, 1,656 km, 4.75 MMSCMD (1.73 bcm/y), IGGL 100%, Guwahati to "Itanagar, Agartala, Imphal". LastUpdated 2025-07-15. No refs besides status.

What I found:
- **Part of it operates.**
  - Guwahati-Numaligarh, 392 km (386 to 396 km depending on source), commissioned 17 July 2025. Sources: CGDIndia, 4 December 2025 (https://cgdindia.net/iggl-to-commission-the-north-east-natural-gas-pipeline-grid-project-by-march-2026/, opened); PNGRB tariff table, which lists 196 km as gas in line pack in the Guwahati-Numaligarh section and a tariff determined 11-06-2025; PNGRB August 2026 report, which shows 392 km operating.
  - Phase I was inaugurated and dedicated by the Prime Minister on 13 March 2026. It is about 553 km across Assam, Arunachal Pradesh and Nagaland. Source: Smart Utilities, 30 March 2026 (https://smartutilities.net.in/2026/03/30/phase-i-of-the-north-east-gas-grid-inaugurated/, opened, gives 553 km and the date only). A search summary lists the sections as Guwahati-Numaligarh, Gohpur-Itanagar (27 km) and Dergaon-Dimapur (128 km) plus ONGC feeders. That section list is snippet only.
  - Dergaon-Dimapur was commissioned 18 August 2026, connecting Nagaland to the grid (News On AIR, 18 August 2026, https://newsonair.gov.in/nagaland-is-now-connected-to-north-east-gas-grid-national-gas-grid-union-minister-hardeep-singh-puri/, opened; Sentinel Assam, 19 August 2026, https://www.sentinelassam.com/north-east-india-news/nagaland-news/nagaland-connected-to-national-gas-grid-as-ongc-iggl-commission-key-gas-infrastructure-2, opened; neither gives the length). ONGC and IGGL also commissioned gas evacuation at Khoraghat the same day.
  - PNGRB's August 2026 report (data as on June 2026) still shows 392 km operating. It predates the 553 km Phase I figure by data date. So operating km is at least 553 km by August 2026 per press, with a PNGRB lag.
- **Not yet built:** PNGRB shows 1,471 km under construction, 1,139 km lowered, 795 km hydrotested (June 2026). A secondary blog (Discovery Alert, 12 September 2026, https://discoveryalert.com/news/nagaland-north-east-gas-grid-september-2026/, opened, secondary source, treat with care) says 88.63 percent physical progress, Phase II of 723 km (Guwahati-Shillong-Silchar, Silchar-Panisagar, Panisagar-Agartala, Aizawl) and Phase III of 394 km (Dimapur-Imphal corridor, Siliguri-Gangtok). Phase naming differs between sources.
- **Imphal and Agartala:**
  - Tripura-Mizoram line, 119.5 km (Panisagar near Agartala to Aizawl), was 45 percent complete in January 2026 with a December 2027 target (Pipeline Journal, 16 January 2026, https://www.pipeline-journal.net/news/tripura-mizoram-natural-gas-pipeline-reaches-45-percent-completion, opened).
  - Dimapur-Imphal: Nagaland overall deadline revised to March 2027, with about 40 percent of the Nagaland work expected by December 2025 (Morung Express, 23 September 2025, https://morungexpress.com/nagaland-pipeline-deadline-revised-to-march-2027, opened).
- **Latest overall target:** the original target was 31 March 2026, now missed. At the Northeast gas conclave on 9 October 2026, IGGL's CEO said only "expected to be completed soon" (PSU Connect, https://www.psuconnect.in/psu-news/iggl-bring-gas-industry-leaders-together-at-northeast-region-gas-conclave-2026, opened). Realistic dates are March 2027 for the Nagaland and Phase II parts and December 2027 for Tripura-Mizoram. I found no official single new date.
- **Length:** the tracker's 1,656 km is the original authorization. PNGRB now lists 1,863 km (August 2026) and the Discovery Alert blog says 1,688 km with 196 km spurs and feeders, including a 175 km Duliajan feeder authorized 20 February 2026. The sources disagree. IGGL conclave coverage says about 1,670 km. Needs a researcher check on which scope to use.
- **Capacity:** tracker 4.75 MMSCMD matches PNGRB January 2026. PNGRB August 2026 shows 5.75 MMSCMD. Update to 5.75 if the researcher accepts the newer table.
- Owner: IGGL (joint venture of IOCL, ONGC, GAIL, Oil India, Numaligarh Refinery) per CGDIndia. The tracker shows IGGL 100 percent. The JV split is not a single owner. Needs a researcher check on whether the owner field should list the five parents.
- Endpoints: Guwahati to Itanagar, Agartala, Imphal is fine as a description. Guwahati to Aizawl, Shillong, Silchar, Gangtok also appear in the scope.

Verdict: **likely wrong** (status and start year). The row mixes an operating 392 to 553 km Phase I with an unfinished remainder.

Suggested change in plain words:
- Split into segments. Mark Guwahati-Numaligarh (392 km, July 2025), Gohpur-Itanagar and Dergaon-Dimapur (Phase I, March 2026 and August 2026) as operating. Keep the remaining 1,100 to 1,300 km as construction.
- StartYear1 2026 is already stale for the remainder. Use 2027 for the Nagaland and Phase II sections and 2027 or later for Tripura-Mizoram. Say a single official date was not found.
- Update capacity to 5.75 MMSCMD and length to match the researcher's chosen scope (1,656 vs 1,863 km).

Source URL check: the status ref (PNGRB data bank page) is a listing page and loads fine. The PNGRB PDF backs up the partial status. Length and capacity have no refs.

## 3. P3602 Mallavaram-Bhopal-Bhilwara-Vijaipur Gas Pipeline, Phase II (GSPL India Transco Ltd, GITL)

Tracker says: construction, StartYear1 2024, Delayed yes, 1,517 km, 78.25 MMSCMD (28.56 bcm/y), owner "Gujarat State Petronet [100%]", start location Mallavaram, no end. Length ref is the PNGRB data bank page. Sister row P0938 Phase I is operating, 365 km, 2021.

What I found:
- **Still not built.** PNGRB shows, for June 2025 and again June 2026, 364 to 365 km operating, 1,516 to 1,517 km under construction, and 0 km lowered, welded or hydrotested. The target date in the PNGRB table is "Mar-20", long past. So the 1,517 km on the tracker equals PNGRB's under-construction (balance) length, and 1,881 minus 365 is the authorized balance. Source: PNGRB August 2026 and June 2025 reports, read from PDF.
- The operating 364 to 365 km is the Phase I line (Kunchanapalli to Ramagundam Fertilizer plant, commissioned 14 October 2019 per HPCL, https://www.hindustanpetroleum.com/pages/City-Gas-Distribution-Networks, opened). The tracker has that as P0938 with a 2021 start year. The HPCL page says 2019. Needs a researcher check on P0938.
- **Real construction progress is nil on the regulator's numbers.** HPCL's page (opened) says "as of May'25, 1342 KM of total scope of 1694 Km has been laid", but this sentence sits in the paragraph on GIGL (Mehsana-Bhatinda and Bhatinda-Gurdaspur), not GITL, so I read it as not about this line. The PNGRB 0 km lowered figure is the better evidence.
- **Start year:** the tracker says 2024, already past. No source I opened gives any current commissioning date. PNGRB's "Mar-20" is obsolete. I found no news of a restart, funding or right-of-use progress in 2025 or 2026. A search summary of a Multibagg item on GITL's Phase I line "network revival" is snippet only and is about the 365 km line. GSPL reportedly said demand is weak without new anchor customers (search snippet only, about 16 months old, not opened).
- **Capacity:** 78.25 MMSCMD matches PNGRB. HPCL page says 77 MMSCMD (21 MMTPA) and 1,881 km at Rs 7,225 crore. Fine.
- **Owner:** GITL is a special purpose vehicle promoted by GSPL with IOCL, BPCL and HPCL (GSPL Transco page, https://gspltrans.com/gitl, opened; HPCL page says HPCL holds an 11 percent stake). The tracker's "Gujarat State Petronet [100%]" is probably wrong. Owner should be GSPL India Transco Ltd, with GSPL as the largest parent and IOCL, BPCL and HPCL as the others. The exact equity split was not on any page I opened. Needs a researcher check.
- **Endpoints:** the end location is blank. Route ends at Bhilwara (Rajasthan) via Bhopal and Vijaipur (HPCL page). Fill in Bhilwara.
- **Length:** 1,517 km for Phase II matches PNGRB balance length. Other sources give 1,677, 1,746 or 2,042 km (including spurs and the Dahod-Ratlam branch), all from search snippets only.

Verdict: **needs researcher check.** Construction is arguably wrong in practice, because PNGRB shows nothing laid, but the project is still authorized and listed as partially commissioned, with no cancellation. The start year is the clearer error.

Suggested change in plain words:
- Consider status "proposed" or "construction" with a note that PNGRB shows 0 km laid as of June 2026. The researcher should decide, since the pipeline has been authorized since 2011 and is not formally cancelled or shelved. A GEM construction status normally needs works under way, and the regulator reports none.
- Do not leave StartYear1 at 2024. Either clear it or mark it unknown, because no source gives a current date.
- Change owner to GSPL India Transco Ltd (joint venture) and fill the end location as Bhilwara.
- Check P0938 start year: HPCL says October 2019, the tracker says 2021.

Source URL check: the Length ref (PNGRB data bank page) loads. The June 2025 and August 2026 PDFs support 1,517 and 1,516 km. Capacity and start have no refs.

## 4. P0922 Ennore-Tuticorn-Bengalaru Gas Pipeline (IOCL)

Tracker says: construction, StartYear1 2025, Delayed yes, 1,431 km, 84.67 MMSCMD (30.90 bcm/y), owner Indian Oil Corp 100%, Ennore to Tuticorin. Status refs are two PNGRB data bank anchors.

What I found:
- **Most of it operates.** PNGRB August 2026 report (data as on June 2026): 1,121 km operating, 310 km under construction, 1,282 km lowered, 1,283 km welded, 1,221 km hydrotested, target date Mar-25 (past). Earlier reports: 1,087 km operating (June 2025 report, 31 March 2025 tariff table) and 1,094 km (August 2025 report, a second table).
- IOCL's own page (Wayback copy of https://iocl.com/gas-pipelines captured 23 March 2025, opened via archive.org) says the pipeline "was commissioned in phases from year 2019-2023 and is 1,084 km long now", with installed capacity 34 MMSCMD. The live iocl.com pages return a redirect loop to curl and WebFetch, so I used the archived copy.
- The Ramanathapuram-Tuticorin spur (143 km) was dedicated in February 2021 (Times of India, via an archived IOCL media page, https://web.archive.org/web/20220525114605/https://iocl.com/MediaDetails/59109, opened). Same page gives the full project as 1,445 km long.
- A News On AIR PIB feature (10 January 2026, https://www.newsonair.gov.in/pragati50-success-of-etbpnmtpl-natural-gas-pipeline-project, opened) says the project is about 1,400 km and "the entire trunkline was commissioned on 25 September 2023", capacity authorized 84.67 MMSCMD, cost over Rs 6,000 crore. That fits the Ennore-Madurai-Tuticorin trunk, about 1,100 km operating. It does not describe a Bengaluru branch.
- **What remains:** the Tiruvallur-Bengaluru section, about 310 km, which is the PNGRB under-construction figure. An IOCL tender for mainline laying and combined station works on the Tiruvallur-Bengaluru section (TBPL, SV-214, Ch 278.2 km) was published 29 December 2025 and closed 19 January 2026 (TenderShark listing, https://www.tendershark.com/details/others-tender/indian-oil-corporation/d839244f-c348-4656-a443-555bf369add8, opened; 275-day work period). That puts the earliest finish at late 2026 to 2027. The inference that the 310 km is the Bengaluru branch is mine. No source states it directly.
- **Start year:** I found no stated target for the Bengaluru section. PNGRB "Mar-25" is obsolete. A tender with a 275-day work period awarded in early 2026 suggests 2027.
- **Capacity:** the tracker's 84.67 MMSCMD is PNGRB's authorized and determined capacity and matches that source. IOCL's own page says 34 MMSCMD installed. Units are MMSCMD in both. Needs a researcher check on which figure the tracker wants for capacity.
- **Length:** 1,431 km matches PNGRB. Other sources say 1,244, 1,400, 1,445 km.
- **Owner:** IOCL, matches.
- **Endpoints:** tracker says Ennore to Tuticorin. The line also goes to Bengaluru, Puducherry, Nagapattinam and Madurai. Consider adding Bengaluru as an end location.
- A search summary of a "Completion Certificate/COD, 1 November 2024" entry on India Investment Grid and DMS Projects was snippet only. It conflicts with PNGRB and the tender, so I did not use it.

Verdict: **likely wrong** as a single construction row. About 1,121 km (78 percent) operates.

Suggested change in plain words:
- Split into segments. Mark the operating trunk (Ennore to Tuticorin via Puducherry, Nagapattinam, Madurai, about 1,100 km, commissioned in phases 2019 to 2023 with the whole trunk reported complete 25 September 2023) as operating. Keep only the Tiruvallur-Bengaluru branch (about 310 km) as construction.
- Change StartYear1 from 2025 (past) to 2027 for the Bengaluru branch, with a note that no official target was found and a 2026 tender is the latest evidence.
- Fix the endpoint to include Bengaluru.

Source URL check: both PNGRB anchors load. The PNGRB PDFs back up 1,431 km, 84.67 MMSCMD and partial status.
