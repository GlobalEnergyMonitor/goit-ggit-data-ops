# MGS III Gas Pipelines (21 construction rows, P6717 to P6737) fact check, 2026-11-19

## Overall

- Status "construction" is right for the program overall. No source says any package is finished or commissioned. Aramco's 2026 reporting says the capacity addition is still due in 2028.
- One partial completion exists. L&T finished a 120 km stretch of 56 inch line in Package 8 (EWPS-8 to EWPS-10), reported by Pipeline & Gas Journal on 9 Oct 2025. About 241 km of that package was still to be built. The package is not complete.
- Package 16 (Jeddah, P6736) was carved out of the main tender and tendered separately. I found no award announcement. "Construction" is unproven for that row.
- Start years: the latest overall target is the first half of 2028 (MEES, 23 Oct 2026, paywalled, only the snippet-level quote was returned by the fetch). Aramco's 2024 release says 2028. Two packages have earlier dates, so 2028 on every row is too late for those (see rows).
- Length: the tracker total of 4,080 km does not match the published package table (4,153 km, MEED Feb 2024 and Blackridge Research Feb 2026). I recomputed both sums. The 73 km gap comes from two row errors: Package 8 is 380 km in the tracker against 480 km published (minus 100), and Jizan is 196 km against 169 km published (plus 27). 4,153 minus 100 plus 27 equals 4,080. Aramco and contractors say "about 4,000 km", so both totals fit that loosely.
- Double counting: the published length for each paired east-west package (3 to 8, 56 inch, "pipelines 3 and 4") is a single figure. The tracker halves it and puts one half on each of lines 3 and 4. So the sum is not inflated by the doubled rows. This only holds if the published figure is the combined length of both lines. No source says so outright. A researcher should confirm whether 56 km, 300 km and so on are combined or per line. If per line, the tracker undercounts the paired segments by half. Sinopec's 696 km for Packages 6 and 7 (OGJ, 10 June 2024) is close to the 710 km combined table figure, which hints these are combined totals, but that is an inference.
- Capacity: Aramco states MGS-3 adds 3.15 billion standard cubic feet per day (bscfd), by 2028, with about 4,000 km of new pipe and 17 compression trains (Aramco press release 30 June 2024, OGJ 1 July 2024, MEES Oct 2026). Blackridge says 3.2 bscfd and a network of 15.7 bscfd, which conflicts with Argus-style reports of 12.5 bscfd after MGS-3; treat those network totals as unverified. No per-package capacity exists anywhere I looked. Suggest carrying 3.15 bscfd at project level only (the P5885 system row, not on each segment), and leave segment capacity blank. Convert at about 0.0283 bcm per bscfd per day, about 32.6 bcm per year for the whole program. Researcher should set the unit convention.
- Could not find: Aramco 2025 annual report text on MGS-3, Aramco Q3 2026 interim report text on MGS-3 (the results release search returned nothing on MGS), any 2026 news on Sinopec, Mapa, Bin Quraya or Max Streicher progress, any award for Package 16.
- Source check: the three source URLs on the rows still open and back up some values (see end). The MEED article names packages and lengths, not completion dates, and is dated 22 Feb 2024, so it supports lengths and "awarded", not "construction".

## Per-row findings

### P6736 Jeddah (52 km, start 2028, status construction)
- Tracker says: construction, 52 km, start 2028.
- Found: 52 km matches the published table. MEED says Package 16 was carved out as a separate tender. Bids were due 30 May 2024 and submitted by July 2024. I found no award or contractor (search snippets only, MEED pages paywalled).
- Verdict: needs researcher check.
- Suggested change: if no award or construction evidence turns up, set status to proposed or announced rather than construction. Ask Aramco or MEED contacts for the award.
- Sources: MEED "Aramco selects contractors for Master Gas System expansion", 22 Feb 2024 (opened). MEED package 16 pieces seen only as search snippets.

### P6733 Riyadh (470 km), P6735 Yanbu-Rabigh (192 km)
- Verdict: correct on length. Kalpataru Projects holds Packages 13, 15 and 17 (NBMCW, 6 Mar 2024, opened: "over 800 km of lateral pipeline", no completion date). 470 + 192 + 169 = 831 km, consistent with "over 800".
- Start 2028 is consistent with the program target; no package-level date found.

### P6737 Jizan (196 km)
- Tracker says 196 km. MEED (22 Feb 2024) and Blackridge (6 Feb 2026) both say 169 km. The Kalpataru "over 800 km" total supports 169 (831 km) and would be 858 km with 196.
- Verdict: likely wrong (probable digit swap).
- Suggested change: set length to 169 km. This lowers the total by 27 km.
- Sources: MEED 22 Feb 2024 and Blackridge Research 6 Feb 2026, both opened.

### P6727 and P6728 EWPS-8 to EWPS-10, lines 3 and 4 (190 km each, 380 km combined)
- Tracker says 190 km each, start 2028.
- Found: Package 8 is 480 km in MEED (22 Feb 2024), OGN (1 May 2025) and Blackridge (6 Feb 2026). Half of 480 is 240 km per line.
- Verdict: likely wrong on length.
- Suggested change: set each line to 240 km (480 combined), subject to the combined versus per-line check above. This adds 100 km to the total.
- Status and timing: L&T completed 120 km of 56 inch pipe under Package 8 (Pipeline & Gas Journal, 9 Oct 2025, opened) and mobilized for 241 km more. OGN's April 2025 schedule showed completion Q4 2025, which is now stale given 241 km remained in Oct 2025. Keep "construction", keep 2028 unless a researcher finds a package date. Note the partly completed stretch.
- Sources: MEED 22 Feb 2024; OGN 1 May 2025; Pipeline & Gas Journal 9 Oct 2025 (all opened). Oil & Gas Middle East "L&T completes Aramco pipeline" returned 403 on fetch and via curl; the claim came from the other page.

### P6723 to P6726 EWPS-4 to EWPS-6 (185 km per line) and EWPS-6 to EWPS-8 (170 km per line), Sinopec
- Tracker says start 2028.
- Found: Sinopec's contract for Packages 6 and 7 states mechanical completion by 31 May 2027, 696 km, SAR 5.2 billion (OGJ, 10 June 2024, opened; contract-time target, may have slipped).
- Verdict: needs researcher check on start year. Lengths match MEED (370 and 340 combined); 696 km versus 710 km is a 14 km difference worth a look.
- Suggested change: consider StartYear 2027 for these four rows, unless later evidence shows delay.

### P6732 Eastern-Qassim (116 km, start 2027)
- Length matches MEED. Gas Arabian won this package. A search summary (not a page I opened) gives contract durations of 30 months for East and Qassim clusters, signed 29 May 2024, which points to about late 2026 or early 2027.
- Verdict: correct or close. Start year 2027 is plausible; a researcher could check whether it is already finished in late 2026. Needs a Gas Arabian Tadawul filing check.
- Sources: Zawya "Gas Arabian bags 2 EPC contracts" (opened, only says $202 million, gives no dates or lengths, so it does not back the start year by itself). Durations: snippet-only claim from Argaam and TradeArabia.

### P6717 and P6718 EWPS-1 to Shedgum, lines 3 and 4 (28 km each, start 2028)
- Length matches MEED (56 km combined). Gas Arabian won this package too. Snippet-only claim: 40 month duration from May 2024, ending about Sept 2027.
- Verdict: needs researcher check. Start year may be 2027.
- Note: the Zawya link cited as the P6717 start reference does not state any date.

### P6719 to P6722 EWPS-1 to EWPS-3 (150 km per line) and EWPS-3 to EWPS-4 (83 km per line)
- Lengths match MEED (300 and 166 km combined). Contractors Mapa and Bin Quraya. No completion date found.
- Verdict: correct on length; start 2028 unverified but consistent with the program target.

### P6729 to P6731 EWJZG-1 (458, 310 and 462 km)
- Lengths match MEED and Blackridge. SICIM's page (opened, undated, lists 2024 to 2028) confirms Package 10 at 310 km under construction, with 6 million injury-free hours reported 13 Nov 2025. So construction is supported for P6730.
- Verdict: correct. No evidence on P6729 and P6731 beyond award. MEED notes Package 9 bids exceeded Aramco's budget and scope changed (snippet only), so needs a quick check that Package 9 was finally awarded to Mapa.

### P6734 Shoaiba (212 km)
- Length matches MEED and SICIM (Package 14, 212 km; SICIM says 56, 10 and 18 inch, tracker says 56, 10 and 16 inch; MEED says 56, 16 and 10). Verdict: correct on length, minor diameter discrepancy (16 versus 18 inch) for the researcher.
- SICIM end of work 2028.
